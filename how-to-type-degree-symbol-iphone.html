Detailed Methods

Method 1: Keyboard Hold Method

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1 Open any app where you can type (Messages, Notes, Mail, etc.)
  2. 2 Tap to bring up the iOS keyboard
  3. 3 Find and tap the 0 (zero) key on the number keyboard
  4. 4 Hold your finger down on the 0 key
  5. 5 A popup menu will appear with the degree symbol (°)
  6. 6 Slide your finger to the degree symbol and release

Pro Tip:

This method works in most iOS apps including Messages, Notes, Mail, Safari, and third-party apps.

Method 2: Text Replacement Setup

Setting Up Text Replacement

  1. 1 Open Settings app on your iPhone
  2. 2 Tap General
  3. 3 Tap Keyboard
  4. 4 Tap Text Replacement
  5. 5 Tap the + button in the top-right corner
  6. 6 In the Phrase field, type or paste the degree symbol (°)
  7. 7 In the Shortcut field, type a short code like "deg" or "degree"
  8. 8 Tap Save in the top-right corner

Usage:

Now when you type your shortcut (e.g., "deg"), it will automatically be replaced with the degree symbol (°).

Method 3: Voice Dictation

Using Siri Dictation

  1. 1 Tap the microphone icon on the keyboard
  2. 2 Say "degree symbol" or "degrees" out loud
  3. 3 iOS will insert the degree symbol (°)

Voice Commands:

  • • "degree symbol" → °
  • • "degrees" → °
  • • "degree Celsius" → °C
  • • "degree Fahrenheit" → °F

Troubleshooting & Common Issues

Degree Symbol Not Available?

  • 1
    Hold duration:

    Make sure you're holding the 0 key long enough (about 1-2 seconds)

  • 2
    Switch to number keyboard:

    Tap the "123" button if you're on the letter keyboard to access the 0 key

  • 3
    iOS version check:

    Ensure your device is running iOS 9 or later (most devices are)

  • 4
    Restart the app:

    Sometimes simply closing and reopening the app fixes keyboard issues

Text Replacement Not Working?

  • 1
    Check Settings:

    Go to Settings > General > Keyboard > Text Replacement and ensure it's enabled

  • 2
    Verify shortcuts:

    Double-check that your shortcuts are properly saved and not duplicated

  • 3
    Avoid conflicts:

    Don't use common words as shortcuts (like "a" or "the")

  • 4
    Restart device:

    A simple restart often fixes text replacement issues

Voice Dictation Issues?

  • 1
    Internet connection:

    Voice dictation requires an internet connection to work

  • 2
    Microphone access:

    Ensure microphone access is enabled for the app you're using

  • 3
    Speak clearly:

    Try speaking "degree symbol" slowly and clearly
